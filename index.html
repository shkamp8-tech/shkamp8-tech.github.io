Complexity emerging from simplicity — through material tension, restrictions and repetition.

My name is Sarrón Harfsterkamp and I am an experimental modular product designer based in Eindhoven. I work through material experimentation and iteration to make physical and functional objects and structures.

Unexpected possibilities from simple rules, material tension and restrictions are what I like to explore — it allows me to surprise myself and others.

First explore, then discover and improve. Starting with material samples, testing different possibilities and iterating on unexpected outcomes is my way of working. Doing physical research first and finding the right balance between complexity and simplicity is a must in every project.

For example: a modular lighting system where one simple shape, repeated through restrictions, creates a complex structure. My research can become lighting sculptures or other functional products that invite people to explore, discover or even interact with something unexpected. Often made with metal, electronics and light.
