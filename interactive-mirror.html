A project about surprise and discovery. One light module directs light into different patterns — the combination of buttons pressed determines which patterns become visible.

Material
Aluminium, mirror, 3D-printed parts
Tech
Servo motors, microcontroller, button input
Context
Fascination project
For me —

An exploration of chaos and order, flux and solid, input and output, repeatability and unpredictability — and the space in between, where one becomes the other. Breaking expectations.

  • Chaos ↔ Order
  • Flux ↔ Solid
  • Input ↔ Output
  • Repeat ↔ Unpredict
Interactive sketchControl → MirrorsTry it ↓

21 buttons.
21 mirrors.
One surprise.

Press the cells on the left — the mirrors on the right change direction. Flux breaks the order.

A simplified model of the object, based on the Voronoi study: every button turns its own mirror towards the light and pushes its neighbours away. Combined, they reinforce or cancel each other — the combination of buttons determines which pattern becomes visible.
